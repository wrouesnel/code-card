Code Card Generator

Choose a secret keyword and use the numbers on the card for its letters as your PIN. An encoded card maps an existing PIN to your keyword. Nothing you enter leaves this page.

Encoded cards are deterministic: the same salt, keyword and code always produce the same card, identical to the code-card encoded command. If you use a random salt, keep a copy of it: you need it to generate the same card again.

Print at 100% scale ("actual size") with no margins, headers or footers so cards are credit card sized. Cards print on an A4 landscape sheet, up to at a time.
